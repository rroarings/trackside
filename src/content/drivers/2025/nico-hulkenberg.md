# The Hulk's heroic podium, a monumental moment

Nico Hulkenberg has brought seasoned expertise to Kick Sauber in 2025, earning 37 points and a podium in Silverstone. His qualifying prowess, edging out teammate Gabriel Bortoleto 8-6, underscores his enduring speed. The German’s return to Sauber, where he raced in 2013, signals his pivotal role in the team’s Audi transition.

At 37, Hulkenberg’s experience and consistency provide stability for Sauber’s evolving project. His ability to deliver points in a midfield car, as seen with a seventh-place finish in Australia, highlights his tactical acumen. Known for his dry humor and professionalism, he remains a respected figure in the paddock.
