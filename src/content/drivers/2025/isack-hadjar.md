# Racing Bulls’ rookie revelation of Hadjar

Isack Hadjar has emerged as a standout rookie for Racing Bulls in 2025, securing 22 points with a career-best sixth-place finish in Monaco. His consistent Q2 appearances and points in six races, including Japan and Spain, highlight his adaptability. The Frenchman’s debut season has sparked talk of a 2026 Red Bull promotion.

The 20-year-old’s speed, honed in Formula 2, has translated well to F1, with a notable recovery from a tearful Australian Grand Prix crash. Hadjar’s ability to tame challenging circuits like Monaco showcases his potential as a future star. His qualifying consistency sets him apart among rookies.
