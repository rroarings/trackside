# Spainish Grand Prix 2025

## Race Highlights

- Oscar Piastri converted his pole position into a lights-to-flag victory, leading a dominant McLaren 1–2 finish
- Lando Norris recovered from a poor start to secure second place, overtaking Max Verstappen on lap 13
- Charles Leclerc finished third for Ferrari after passing Verstappen during a frantic late-race restart
- Nico Hülkenberg secured a season-best fifth place for Sauber, overtaking Lewis Hamilton on the penultimate lap
- Max Verstappen dropped to 10th place following a 10-second penalty for causing a collision with George Russell in the closing stages
- Kimi Antonelli suffered his second engine failure in three races while running in seventh place, triggering a late-race Safety Car
- Fernando Alonso scored his first points of the 2025 season by finishing ninth in his home race

## Race Summary

Oscar Piastri continued his championship momentum at the 2025 Spanish Grand Prix, securing his fifth win of the season. While Piastri controlled the race from the front, his teammate Lando Norris had to fight back after losing position to Max Verstappen at the start. Verstappen initially took the lead through an aggressive undercut strategy during the first round of pit stops, but his race eventually unraveled due to a gamble on tyre strategy and late-race incidents.

The final ten laps were defined by high drama following a safety car period caused by Kimi Antonelli's engine failure. At the restart, Verstappen, struggling on the hard tyre compound, was overtaken by Charles Leclerc and George Russell. A frustrated Verstappen then collided with Russell at Turn 5 while trying to reclaim the position, resulting in a time penalty that plummeted the defending champion to the edge of the points.

Behind the podium finishers, Nico Hülkenberg delivered one of the standout performances of the day by guiding his Sauber to fifth place, holding off a charging Lewis Hamilton. This result helped Ferrari overtake both Mercedes and Red Bull for second in the Constructors' Championship. Meanwhile, Piastri extended his lead in the Drivers' Championship to ten points over Norris as the season moves toward the North American leg.
