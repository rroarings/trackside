# Cadillac

Cadillac makes its debut in the 2025 Formula 1 season as the newest manufacturer on the grid. The team brings significant engineering resources and a development programme oriented around efficiency and reliability.

## Season Preview

Cadillac will enter the championship with a measured approach, aiming for steady development across the season. Rookie driver pairing Evan Hart and Leo Martin will be tasked with collecting data and helping refine the CAD-01 for future seasons.
