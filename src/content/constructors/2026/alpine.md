# Alpine

Alpine has had an up-and-down 2025, with flashes of speed offset by crashes and strategic missteps in a fiercely competitive midfield.

## Team Information

**Founded:** 2021
**Base:** Enstone, United Kingdom / Viry-Châtillon, France  
**Engine:** Renault

## Current Form

Alpine sits **10th** with **20 points**. Esteban Ocon’s P9 in Mexico helped, but Pierre Gasly and Franco Colapinto have been involved in costly incidents—Colapinto’s hard-tyre start gamble in Mexico failed dramatically. The team shows pace in qualifying but lacks race-day execution. With Renault confirming a Mercedes switch for 2026, 2025 is a transitional year with survival in mind.
