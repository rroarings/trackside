## Current Form

Aston Martin holds **7th** with **69 points**. Fernando Alonso’s experience yielded a Bahrain podium, but both he and Lance Stroll have struggled on low-grip tracks. No podiums since mid-season, and development has stalled. The team announced Jack Crawford as 2026 reserve driver, signaling future focus. A quiet Mexico (P11/P13) reflects their current stagnation.
