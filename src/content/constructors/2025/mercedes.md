# Mercedes

The Silver Arrows have delivered a solid, if unspectacular, 2025 campaign, locked in a tight battle for second in the Constructors’ standings.

## Team Information

**Founded:** 2010  
**Base:** Brackley, United Kingdom  
**Championships:** 8 Constructors’, 9 Drivers’
**Engine:** Mercedes

## Current Form

Mercedes holds **3rd** with **355 points**, just 1 point behind Ferrari after Mexico. George Russell’s four podiums and Kimi Antonelli’s impressive rookie season (P7 in Drivers’) have kept them competitive, especially on high-speed tracks. A controversial team orders call in Mexico frustrated both drivers. Under minor cost cap scrutiny for a procedural breach, the team focuses on development for 2026. Antonelli’s radio outbursts signal growing pains but highlight his fiery potential.
