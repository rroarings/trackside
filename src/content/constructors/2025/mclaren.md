# McLaren

The historic British team McLaren has returned to competitiveness in recent years, with their young driver pairing showing great promise for the future.

## Team Information

**Founded:** 1966  
**Base:** Woking, United Kingdom  
**Championships:** 8 Constructors’, 12 Drivers’  
**Engine:** Mercedes

## Current Form

McLaren has dominated the 2025 season, clinching the **Constructors’ Championship** with six races to spare after a remarkable run of 16 wins from 20 races. Lando Norris (7 wins) and Oscar Piastri (9 wins) have shared the spotlight, with Norris taking the Drivers’ lead by 1 point after a commanding pole-to-flag victory in Mexico. The team’s consistency is unmatched—scoring in every race—and their upgraded floor package has made the MCL39 the class of the field. Intra-team tension remains a subplot, but performance remains untouchable as they head to Brazil.
