# Williams

Williams has shown resilience in 2025, leveraging Alex Albon’s consistency while navigating Carlos Sainz’s integration and occasional setbacks.

## Team Information

**Founded:** 1977  
**Base:** Grove, United Kingdom  
**Championships:** 9 Constructors’, 7 Drivers’  
**Engine:** Mercedes

## Current Form

Williams sits **5th** with **111 points**. Albon’s steady points haul (P8 in Drivers’) has been the backbone, while Sainz has contributed despite penalties—a 5-place grid drop and pit-lane speeding violation in Mexico led to retirement. A bizarre sensor failure from Lap 1 contact compounded issues. The team remains competitive in the midfield but needs cleaner weekends to stay above Racing Bulls below.
