# Red Bull Racing

Red Bull Racing has endured a turbulent 2025, slipping to fourth in the Constructors’ Championship amid reliability woes and mid-season lineup changes.

## Team Information

**Founded:** 2005  
**Base:** Milton Keynes, United Kingdom  
**Championships:** 6 Constructors’, 7 Drivers’  
**Engine:** Honda RBPT  

## Current Form

Red Bull sits **4th** with **346 points**, in fourth place entering Mexico, Verstappen’s five wins (including a US Sprint and GP double) have kept them in contention, but Yuki Tsunoda's pace since his early-season swap with Liam Lawson have hurt. The team has delayed 2026 lineup decisions to focus on his title defense.
