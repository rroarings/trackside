# Ferrari

The iconic Italian marque Ferrari has mounted a strong challenge in 2025, reclaiming second in the Constructors’ standings by a single point after a dramatic Mexico weekend.

## Team Information

**Founded:** 1950  
**Base:** Maranello, Italy  
**Championships:** 16 Constructors’, 15 Drivers’  
**Engine:** Ferrari  

## Current Form

Ferrari sits **2nd** with **356 points**, powered by Charles Leclerc’s podium consistency and Lewis Hamilton’s adaptation in his debut season. Leclerc’s P2 in Mexico and Hamilton’s P8 (despite a 10-second penalty) secured the position over Mercedes. The team has three double-podiums and two wins (Imola, Spa), but early-season reliability issues cost ground. Hamilton skipped FP1 for rookie Dino Beganovic under FIA rules. With upgrades planned for Brazil, Ferrari remains a dark horse in the Drivers’ fight.
